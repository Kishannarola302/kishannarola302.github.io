Privacy Policy

Palestack: Card Sort Puzzle (iPhone and iPad) · Effective date: November 24, 2026

Summary

Palestack: Card Sort Puzzle ("the game") is a puzzle game. It has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location. The game shows ads from Google AdMob. This policy explains what information is involved and the choices you have.

Who we are

The game is made and published by Krushna Narola, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact: narolakishan19@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Your level, star ratings, coin balance, unlocked themes, booster counts and settings are saved only on your device so you can continue playing. We do not run servers and we do not receive a copy of this information. Deleting the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform to show ads and to ask for consent where the law requires it. To serve and measure ads, analyze ad and app performance, limit how often you see an ad, make the consent tool work, remember your consent choice and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Device information such as device type and system version, and device identifiers; your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking
  • Approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address
  • Advertising data, such as which ads you saw or tapped
  • Product interaction data, such as app launches and ad views
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash and performance data

We do not receive or keep this information ourselves. Google, acting as an independent provider, may share ad-request data with the advertising partners it works with to run ad auctions, measure ads and prevent fraud. Where Google's consent message is shown, these partners are listed in it. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We only use third-party services, such as Google, that are bound by their own privacy policies and that provide the same or equal protection of user data as described in this policy.

We do not sell your information for money. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier and ad data to show personalized ads, which some laws treat as "sharing"; you can stop this at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → Privacy & Security → Tracking or, where it is shown, in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Apart from the data Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects as described above (which Google may share with its advertising partners), we do not share data with any other third party.

Your choices and how to withdraw consent

  • Tracking: the game asks for permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency before the advertising identifier can be used. You can change this at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Nothing in the game depends on allowing tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and other regions where required): a consent message from Google is shown on first launch. You can review or withdraw your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • US state privacy rights: if you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier for personalized ads across apps, which some US state laws call "sharing". To opt out, turn tracking off in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices where it is shown.
  • Advertising identifier: when tracking is off, apps receive only a blank advertising identifier (all zeros), so it cannot be used to track you.
  • Optional videos: watching a video for coins or a booster is always your choice. Coins have no real-world value, cannot be bought with money and cannot be transferred.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the game. Google keeps information it collects for ads only for limited periods, described in How Google retains data. Ads data is linked to device identifiers, not to an account. The most effective way to remove that link is to turn tracking off in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → Privacy & Security → Tracking and to withdraw consent in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. You can also contact Google, or email us and we will help.

If you email us

If you contact us or send an ad report, your email app sends us your email address and what you write. An ad report also includes the reason you chose, the time, the screen or level and the app version. We use this only to reply and to review the ad, we never share it, and we keep it only as long as needed to handle your request.

Reporting an ad

If you see an inappropriate ad, use Settings → Report an Ad in the game, or tap the ⓘ (AdChoices) icon on the ad to hide or report it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Ad Privacy Choices), and to complain to your local data protection authority. Apart from emails you send us, we do not hold personal data about you. To access or delete your emails with us, email narolakishan19@gmail.com. For ads data, use the choices above, contact Google, or email us and we will help.

Children

The game is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
