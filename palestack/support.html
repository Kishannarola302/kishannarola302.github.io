How to play

  • Move cards: tap a stack to pick up its top cards of one color, then tap a stack with the same top color or an empty slot.
  • Clear a stack: put 10 cards of one color in one slot. They burst, give coins and free the slot.
  • Mystery cards (?): face-down cards flip open when the card above them is moved.
  • Locked slots: a lock shows a color. Clear that color and the slot opens.
  • Zen Fever: make 5 quick matches in a row for 10 seconds of double coins.
  • Boosters: Undo your last move, add one Extra Slot per level, or Shuffle the face-up cards into a fresh layout. When a booster runs out, get one with coins or by choosing to watch a short video.

Frequently asked questions

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level is checked by a solver before you play it. If you get stuck, use Undo, Shuffle or Extra Slot, or retry the level.

Does the game have ads?

Yes. A small banner sits below the game, a short ad may appear between some levels (never during play), and you can choose to watch a video for coins or a booster. There are no in-app purchases.

How do I get more coins?

Clear stacks, finish levels and hit Zen Fever. You can also tap the coin (+) button and choose to watch a short video for 50 coins, up to 10 times a day.

How do I report an ad?

In the game open Settings → Report an Ad, or tap the ⓘ icon on the ad itself.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices in the game (shown where required by law), and the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Yes. Open Settings in the game to switch Sound Effects, Music & Ambience, Haptic Feedback and Colorblind Symbols on or off.

Do I need the internet?

No. The game works offline. Ads and videos need a connection.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved only on your device. Deleting the game or erasing your device without restoring a backup removes it.
