Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Each arrow can slide only in the direction it points. Tap an arrow whose path to the edge is clear and it slides off the board. Clear every arrow to win the level. You have 3 lives (hearts) for each try. Tapping a blocked arrow costs one. If you run out, you can try again, watch a video or spend 150 coins for one more life. There is no timer and no waiting for lives to refill. "How to play" in the game's Settings or in the Pause menu shows the rules again and lets you replay the tutorial.

When do the Daily and Weekend challenges open?

The Daily Challenge unlocks at level 10 and has a new puzzle every day (40 coins the first time you clear it). Missed a day? You can still play earlier days from the calendar. Finish every day of a month to win that month's trophy and 300 coins.

The Weekend Challenge unlocks at level 20 and runs from Friday until the end of Sunday (your iPhone's time). Clear its 60 levels in order. Each one gives 20 coins. When the weekend ends you also win a prize for the highest tier you reached: 5 levels = 50 coins, 15 = 250, 30 = 500, 50 = 1,000. You claim it the next time you open the game.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone automatically. There is no account and no in-game cloud save, so it is removed if you delete the game. Like other app data, it is part of your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backup, so it comes with you if you set up a new iPhone from a backup or by direct transfer.

How do I turn off sound, music or vibration?

Open Settings in the game (the sliders button at the top right of the Home screen) and tap Sound, Music or Vibration to turn it off. During a level you can also tap Pause (top right) and use the three buttons there. Music also stops on its own while another app is playing audio. Sound effects still play.

How do I get more coins or hints?

Coins come from clearing levels (including Daily and Weekend levels), the free gift in the Shop (every 4 hours), the Daily Reward (the gift button on Home or in the Shop), chapter chests, monthly trophies, achievements and Weekend Challenge prizes. Hints come from the Daily Reward and chapter chests, and you can trade coins for hints in the Shop. You can also choose to watch a reward video for 50 coins (up to 10 a day) or a free hint, or to double the coins from a level, a chest or the Daily Reward. There are no purchases in the game.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the game's Settings also shows a "Privacy choices" row that opens Google's consent form (internet connection needed). See the Privacy Policy for details.

The daily reminder does not appear.

The reminder arrives once a day at 7 pm (your iPhone's time) and does not appear while the game is open. Turn on "Daily reminder" in the game's Settings and allow notifications for Flechio in iOS Settings > Notifications. If notifications are off for Flechio, the switch will not stay on.
