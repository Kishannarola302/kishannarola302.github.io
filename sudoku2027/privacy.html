Privacy Policy

Sudoku 2027: Brain Numbers (iPhone) · Effective date: November 5, 2026

Sudoku 2027 ("the game") is made and published by Krushna Narola, an individual developer ("we", "us"). You can contact us at narolakishan19@gmail.com. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we do not run any servers, the game has no account of its own, and your game progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information for those ads as described below. Game Center is optional and is run by Apple.

1. Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off. We never receive it.

  • Game progress: puzzles in progress, hints, statistics (puzzles played and won, times, perfect games), your streak, daily puzzle results, monthly trophies, event points, badges, unlocked themes and avatars, finished lessons and the daily gift.
  • Settings: gameplay options, sound, music, vibration, theme, number size and reminder settings.
  • Your player name and avatar. Until you type a name, the game shows an automatic one such as "Player 1234". They are shown only inside the game on your device and are never sent to us, to Google or to Game Center (Game Center shows your own Game Center nickname, see section 5).

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets read a small summary of this information (such as your streak and daily puzzle progress) from the same iPhone. Deleting the game deletes this information. We keep no copy, so progress cannot be restored by us. Like any app's data, it may be included in an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone that you make yourself.

2. Information we collect

We do not run servers and do not receive or store personal information ourselves. The ads software in the game (Google AdMob) does collect the information listed in section 3, and this is what the App Store privacy label for Sudoku 2027 shows. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and the game has no chat. Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center (see section 5). The Share buttons only open the iOS share sheet with a short text about your result (for example your time, statistics or a trophy; it never includes your player name). It is sent only if you pick an app or person to send it to, and we do not receive it.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and anything you put in your message, and we use it only to answer you and to act on what you wrote about (for example, to block an ad you reported). Our email address is a Gmail account, so your message is stored and handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, on servers that may be in the United States or other countries. Google uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for this. We also see summary reports from Google AdMob and Apple, such as how many ads were shown or how many people downloaded the game in each country. These reports do not identify you.

3. Advertising by Google AdMob

The game is free and is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the puzzle screen, an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a puzzle (never during play, not before your third win and at most once every three minutes), and reward videos that you choose to watch (for example for a free hint, a second chance, a bigger reward or a short ad-free break).

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland), using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Some ads may come from other advertising companies that buy ad space through Google (Google-certified ad technology providers). To serve, measure and, where allowed, personalise ads, Google and these companies may use device identifiers and similar technologies. Google's software may also store information on your device, such as identifiers and your consent choices, in a way similar to cookies. Where consent is required, Google's consent form lists these companies and lets you choose.

To show ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, personalise ads to your interests, Google may collect and process the information below. Google also uses it for analytics and to maintain and improve its advertising services. Google's consent software also uses your IP address to work out your general region, so it knows whether a privacy message must be shown to you, and it remembers your answer on your device. This information includes:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Information about how you use the game and its ads, such as app launches, ads shown, ad taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, app launch time and other performance data about the ads software.

Ads in the game are limited to content Google rates as suitable for general audiences (rating "G").

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We only allow third parties that provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and as required by Apple's App Store rules to receive information from the game. Today that means Google and the advertising partners Google allows to bid for ad space in the game under Google's policies, and Apple for Game Center leaderboards if you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone (see section 5). No one else receives information from the game. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

4. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Tracking permission. Before the IDFA can be used, iOS asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using cross-app tracking. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent and privacy choices. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, Google's consent message appears the first time you reach the Home screen (if you were offline, it appears when you are back online while the game is open, or the next time you start the game). If you live in a US state whose privacy law lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, no message appears on its own; you can open Google's opt-out form yourself from the Privacy choices option. In all of these regions you can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Help & about > Privacy choices. This option appears only in those regions and needs an internet connection.
  • Reward videos are always optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

5. Game Center (optional)

The game can use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards. If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the game sends your scores to Game Center: the number of puzzles you solved (this week and in total) and your time for today's daily puzzle. Apple shows these scores with your Game Center nickname to other Game Center players on the leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple Account details. You can sign out of Game Center or limit who sees your profile in iOS Settings > Game Center. If you do not use Game Center, nothing is sent.

6. Notifications

Reminders are off until you turn them on. If you turn on the daily puzzle reminder, the game asks iOS for permission and schedules one reminder a day on your device, at the time you choose. If you ask to be told about new events, it schedules a reminder when the next monthly event starts. They are not sent from a server and contain no personal information. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings > Notifications.

7. Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Hints, themes and avatars are earned by playing or by choosing to watch reward videos. They have no real-world value.

8. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it in a portable format. Because we hold no personal information about you apart from any emails you send us, most requests are handled through the choices in section 4, by deleting the game, or directly with Google at policies.google.com/privacy. You are always welcome to contact us.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the legal basis for personalised ads, and for storing or reading identifiers on your device, is your consent, given in Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Help & about > Privacy choices; this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it. If you do not consent, Google may still show limited or non-personalised ads. Where the law allows, Google may rely on legitimate interests for purposes such as fraud prevention and ad measurement. When you email us, our legal basis for using your email address and message is our legitimate interest in answering you, or our legal obligation when your message is a privacy request. Google may process information in the United States and other countries, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

We do not sell personal information for money. However, some US state privacy laws (such as California's) treat letting Google and its advertising partners collect information for personalised, cross-context behavioural advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. In the past 12 months, the following categories may have been "sold" or "shared" in this sense with Google and the advertising partners Google allows to bid for ad space in the game: identifiers (such as the Identifier for Vendors, the IDFA if you allow tracking, and IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (device and app information and how you use the game and its ads), and approximate location estimated from your IP address. You can opt out at any time with Privacy choices in the game (Settings > Help & about > Privacy choices; it is shown in the US states where these laws apply and needs an internet connection). It opens Google's opt-out form and tells Google to restrict how it uses your information for ads. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking) also stops the IDFA from being used for cross-app tracking. Because we do not receive any information that identifies you or your device, we cannot apply an opt-out for you from an email, but if you write to us we will reply within 15 business days and help you use these settings. We do not have actual knowledge that we sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16. We do not collect sensitive personal information. You may use an authorised agent to make a request for you by emailing us; we may ask the agent to show that you gave them permission. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

10. Security, retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device and we hold no copy. You can delete it at any time by deleting the game. Google's ads software sends information over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to help you, and we delete it within 12 months of our last reply, or sooner if you ask.

How to withdraw consent or ask for deletion:

  • Choose "Ask App Not to Track", or turn tracking off in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Where it is shown, change your ad choices in the game under Settings > Help & about > Privacy choices.
  • Delete the game. This deletes all game data on your device and stops any further collection.
  • Email narolakishan19@gmail.com to have any emails you sent us deleted.
  • To ask Google about data it holds, use the options in the Google Privacy Policy. For Game Center, use iOS Settings > Game Center or contact Apple.

We reply to privacy requests within 30 days.
