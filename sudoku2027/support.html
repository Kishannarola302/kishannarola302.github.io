Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Fill every empty cell so that each row, each column and each box contains every number exactly once (1 to 9, or 1 to 6 in Mini 6×6). Tap a cell, then tap a number. Turn on Notes to write small reminder numbers. In Killer Sudoku the numbers in each dotted cage must also add up to the small number in its corner, and no number repeats inside a cage. The Learn tricks screen has short lessons with real puzzles.

What do the mistake limit and the other settings do?

With "Show mistakes right away" on, a wrong number turns red and counts as a mistake. With the mistake limit also on, the third mistake ends the game, but you can watch a short video for a second chance, start over or pick a new game. With "Show mistakes right away" off, wrong numbers are not marked and the mistake limit does not apply. You can switch both off in Settings, along with the highlights, the timer and other options.

How do daily puzzles and trophies work?

There is a new daily puzzle every day (by your iPhone's date). Solving today's puzzle gives a free hint. Solve 20 daily puzzles in one month to win that month's trophy and 5 hints. You can also play earlier days from the calendar.

What are events?

Every month has an event. Solving puzzles gives event points (more for harder puzzles). At 10, 20, 30 and 40 points you get hints, an event avatar, an event board theme and a final reward.

How do I get more hints?

Hints come from the daily gift, today's daily puzzle, monthly trophies, events, badges and lessons. You can also choose to watch a short video for a free hint (up to 5 a day). There are no purchases in the game.

Is my progress saved? Can I move it to a new iPhone?

Progress is saved on your iPhone automatically. There is no account and no in-game cloud save, so it is removed if you delete the game. Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on, and it comes with you when you set up a new iPhone from that backup or by transferring directly from your old iPhone.

How do leaderboards work?

Leaderboards use Apple's Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings, or tap "Connect Game Center" on the Me screen. Your daily puzzle time and the number of puzzles you solve are then shown with your Game Center nickname.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add Sudoku 2027. Widgets show your streak, today's puzzle and the game you are playing.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Tracking: iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In regions where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states such as California), the game's Settings > Help & about also shows a "Privacy choices" row. It opens Google's privacy form, where you can change your ad consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland) or opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (US states). An internet connection is needed. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report an ad?

If an ad looks inappropriate or not suitable for all ages, open Settings > Help & about > Report an ad in the game, or email us at the address above, and tell us what it showed, with a screenshot if you can. We review reports and block that ad where we can.

What if the daily reminder does not appear?

Turn on "Daily puzzle reminder" in the game's Settings and allow notifications for Sudoku 2027 in iOS Settings > Notifications. Each time you leave the game, it plans your reminders for the next 7 days, so they stop if you do not open the game for a week or more. Open the game once to start them again. The reminder skips the current day once you have solved today's puzzle.
