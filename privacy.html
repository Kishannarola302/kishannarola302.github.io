Privacy Policy

Flechio: Arrow Logic Puzzle (iPhone) · Effective date: November 3, 2026

Flechio ("the game") is made and published by Krushna Narola, an individual developer ("we", "us"). You can contact us at narolakishan19@gmail.com. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have.

In short: we do not run any servers, the game has no account or sign-in, and your game progress stays on your iPhone. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect some information for those ads as described below.

1. Information that stays on your device

The game saves the following on your iPhone so you can continue where you left off. We never receive it.

  • Game progress: levels, perfect levels, coins, hints, daily and weekend challenge results, trophies, achievements, the Daily Reward and free gift timers, and statistics such as total play time, best streak and arrows freed.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, language, gameplay options and the daily reminder setting.
  • Your Profile name and avatar colour. Until you type a name, the game shows an automatic one such as "Player 1234". They are shown only inside the game on your device.

Deleting the game deletes this information. The game has no cloud save and we keep no copy, so progress cannot be restored after deleting the game. Like any app's data, it may be included in a full iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone that you make yourself.

2. Information we collect

We do not run servers and do not receive or store personal information ourselves. The ads software in the game (Google AdMob) does collect the information listed in section 3, and this is what the App Store privacy label for Flechio shows. We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own, and the game has no sign-in, chat or social features.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and anything you put in your message (for example your iPhone model, iOS version or a screenshot), and we use it only to answer you. We also see summary reports from Google AdMob and Apple, such as how many ads were shown or how many people downloaded the game in each country. These reports do not identify you.

3. Advertising by Google AdMob

The game is free and is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen while you play a level, an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never during play), and reward videos that you choose to watch (for example for extra coins, a hint, an extra life or double rewards).

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and Switzerland), using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Some ads may come from other advertising companies that buy ad space through Google (Google-certified ad technology providers). To serve, measure and, where allowed, personalise ads, Google and these companies may use device identifiers and similar technologies. Google's software may also store information on your device, such as identifiers and your consent choices, in a way similar to cookies. Where consent is required, Google's consent form lists these companies and lets you choose.

To show ads, measure how they perform, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you allow it, personalise ads to your interests, Google may collect and process the information below. Google also uses it for analytics and to maintain and improve its advertising services. Google's consent software also uses your IP address to work out your general region, so it knows whether a privacy message must be shown to you, and it remembers your answer on your device. This information includes:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Vendors and, only if you allow tracking, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA).
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (not precise) location.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language.
  • Information about how you use the game and its ads, such as app launches, ads shown, ad taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs, app launch time and other performance data about the ads software.

Ads in the game are limited to content Google rates as suitable for general audiences (rating "G").

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We only allow third parties that provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and as required by Apple's App Store rules to receive information from the game. Today that means Google and the advertising partners Google allows to bid for ad space in the game under Google's policies. No one else receives information from the game. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

4. Your choices about ads and tracking

  • Tracking permission. Before the IDFA can be used, iOS asks whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalised using cross-app tracking. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Consent and privacy choices. If you are in a region where the law requires it (for example the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), Google's privacy message appears after the welcome screen the first time you start the game, or later if you were offline. You can review or change your choice at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices. This option appears only in those regions and needs an internet connection.
  • Reward videos are always optional. You never have to watch one to keep playing.

5. Game engine

The game is made with the Unity engine. Unity's optional data services (Unity Analytics, crash reporting and performance reporting) are turned off in this game, and we do not use Unity to collect information about you or how you play. If the engine sends Unity any limited technical information about the device (such as device model, operating system version or graphics hardware) for engine support, it does not identify you and we do not receive it. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

6. Notifications

The daily reminder is off until you turn it on. When you do, the game asks iOS for permission and schedules one reminder each evening at 7 pm on your device. It is not sent from a server and contains no personal information. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings > Notifications.

7. Purchases

The game has no in-app purchases. Coins and hints are earned by playing or by choosing to watch reward videos. They have no real-world value.

8. Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we hold no personal information about you apart from any emails you send us, most requests are handled through the choices in section 4, by deleting the game, or directly with Google at policies.google.com/privacy. You are always welcome to contact us.

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the legal basis for personalised ads, and for storing or reading identifiers on your device, is your consent, given in Google's consent form. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Where the law allows, Google may rely on legitimate interests for purposes such as fraud prevention and ad measurement. Google may process information in the United States and other countries, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, some US state privacy laws (such as California's) may treat letting Google collect device identifiers and ad information for personalised, cross-context behavioural advertising as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt out at any time by choosing "Ask App Not to Track" (iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking), by using Settings > Privacy choices in the game where it is shown, or by emailing us. We act on opt-out requests within 15 business days. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

10. Security, retention and deletion

Your game data stays on your device and we hold no copy. You can delete it at any time by deleting the game. Google's ads software sends information over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policy (policies.google.com/technologies/retention). If you email us, we keep your message only as long as we need it to help you, and we delete it within 12 months of our last reply, or sooner if you ask.

How to withdraw consent or ask for deletion:

  • Choose "Ask App Not to Track", or turn tracking off in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Where it is shown, change your ad choices in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Delete the game. This deletes all game data on your device and stops any further collection.
  • Email narolakishan19@gmail.com to have any emails you sent us deleted.
  • To ask Google about data it holds, use the options in the Google Privacy Policy.

We reply to privacy requests within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
